## Production Guidelines for Image-to-Video

**Veo 3.1 Image-to-Video Settings:**
- **Starting Frame:** Use provided reference images as first frame
- **Camera Movement:** Subtle movements (push in, tilt, pan) to maintain realism
- **Duration:** 8-12 seconds for smooth, professional b-roll
- **Motion Intensity:** Medium (avoid excessive movement that breaks from photo realism)
- **Consistency:** Maintain lighting and weather conditions from source image

**Technical Specifications:**
- **Resolution:** 4K (3840x2160)
- **Frame Rate:** 24fps for cinematic quality
- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 for web and presentation use
- **Color Grading:** Match source images - vibrant blues, warm oranges, natural daylight
- **Stabilization:** Smooth gimbal-style movements

**Thematic Focus:**
1. **Government Partnership:** Emphasize Gombe Municipality as first implementation site
2. **National Identity:** Showcase Kinshasa landmarks as symbols of progress
3. **Postal Modernization:** Highlight transformation of mail delivery services
4. **Urban Infrastructure:** Demonstrate organized, navigable city streets
5. **Digital Integration:** Show seamless blend of physical and digital systems

- ✅ Commune de Gombe (orange & blue municipal building)

- ✅ Boulevard du 30 Juin (main commercial boulevard)
